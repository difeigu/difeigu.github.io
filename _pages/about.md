I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Rutgers University](https://www.rutgers.edu/) (expected 2028), advised by [Prof. Dimitris Metaxas](https://www.cs.rutgers.edu/faculty/dimitris-n-metaxas/). My research focuses on **vision-language models**, **medical imaging**, **explainability**, and **agentic AI**.

I am currently a Research Intern at [Siemens Healthineers](https://www.siemens-healthineers.com/) – Digital Technology & Innovation (DTI) in Princeton, NJ, where I work on an agentic autoresearch framework for self-improving adaptation of medical foundation models (best intern poster award). Previously, I was a Research Intern at the Centre for Perceptual and Interactive Intelligence (CPII) in Hong Kong (2021–2023), and I received my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Co-operative Program) from the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/) (2016–2021).
